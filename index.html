Our Roots As Nossas Raízes

For more than 100 years, the Irmandade do Bom Jesus Milagroso has been a home on the hill for Madeiran Portuguese families in Hayward. It was built by people who worked hard, prayed together, and never missed a chance to celebrate.

  1. 1921 The Irmandade is founded.
  2. 1922 Officially incorporated on March 31. The land for our hall is given for a $10 gold coin.
  3. 1978 A fire destroys the original hall.
  4. 1980–81 Our members rebuild. The hall you see today is the result.
  5. 2022 100 years! We celebrate with a Centennial Festa in September.

Now it's our children's turn. A próxima geração is the future of the hall. Bring your kids and grandkids to a festa, help set up, and make the same happy memories we did.

A Hall for Everyone

Families of every background celebrate here: weddings, birthdays, quinceañeras, receptions, and church services. We proudly fly the flags of Portugal and the United States, and everyone is welcome. Todos são bem-vindos.
