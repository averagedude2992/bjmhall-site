For more than 100 years, the Irmandade do Bom Jesus Milagroso has been a home on the hill for Madeiran Portuguese families in Hayward. It was built by people who worked hard, prayed together, and never missed a chance to celebrate.

  1. 1921

    The Irmandade is founded.

  2. 1922

    Officially incorporated on March 31. The land for our hall is given for a $10 gold coin.

  3. 1939

    A high-altitude aerial photo already shows buildings on the hall's corner of Prospect Street (now Ocean View Drive) and Sycamore Street (now Ash Street).

  4. 1950s

    The Bom Jesus festa draws thousands. Newspapers estimate 2,000 to 5,000 people. Processions go from the IDES Hall on C Street to All Saints Church, then to Bom Jesus Park at Ash and Prospect for sopas and carne, a barbecue, a dance, and an auction. In 1953 the papers describe a shrine to the Bom Jesus on the grounds.

  5. 1958

    A county property map labels the corner lot Irmandade do Bom Jesus Milagroso Corp.

  6. 1959

    Prospect Street becomes Ocean View Drive. Besides the Bom Jesus festa, the society also holds Our Lady of the Monte and Our Lady of Loretto festivals.

  7. 1978

    An arson fire guts the original 1½-story frame stucco hall in the 21100 block of Ocean View Drive, then just outside Hayward city limits. The fire chief calls it a total loss.

  8. 1980–81

    Our members rebuild. The hall you see today is the result.

  9. 2022

    100 years! We celebrate with a Centennial Festa in September.

Now it's our children's turn. A próxima geração is the future of the hall. Bring your kids and grandkids to a festa, help set up, and make the same happy memories we did.

A Hall for Everyone

Families of every background celebrate here: weddings, birthdays, quinceañeras, receptions, and church services. We proudly fly the flags of Portugal and the United States, and everyone is welcome. Todos são bem-vindos.

Maximum capacity: 250 guests. Capacidade máxima: 250 pessoas.
